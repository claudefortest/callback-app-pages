Callback lets Claude place phone calls to you. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Phone number. The number you give us so calls can reach you.
  • Call data. The time and length of each call, and the reason Claude called. If you turn on transcripts, we also keep what was said.
  • Task details. The tasks you hand off and their results, so we can tell you about them.
  • Basic technical data. Device type and error logs, used to keep the service running.

How we use it

We use your data to place calls, run your tasks, keep your account secure, and fix problems. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

We use Google sign-in only to identify you. Our use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

We share data only with providers that help us run Callback: a telephony provider to connect calls, an AI provider to power Claude, and a host to store data. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. Call transcripts are deleted after 30 days unless you delete them sooner. After you close your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.

Your choices

  • Turn call transcripts on or off at any time.
  • Export or delete your data from your account settings.
  • Stop calls any time by pausing Callback or removing your number.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t promise absolute protection.

Children

Callback is not for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@example.com.
